Learn everyday Modern Hebrew the natural way — by chatting with a friendly AI tutor that only uses words you know, one new word at a time.

Vowels or no vowels?

Hebrew can be written with little vowel dots (נִקּוּד) or without. Change it any time in Settings.

One Hebrew thing 🇮🇱

Hebrew changes depending on whether you're a boy or a girl (the words for "you", verb endings, etc.). This helps the tutor teach you the right forms.

A little about you

So your tutor can chat about things you like. Both optional.

Connect your AI 🔌

Yalla uses OpenAI to power the tutor. Paste an OpenAI key once — it's saved only on this device and never shared.

🔑 Where's this from? platform.openai.com → API keys → "Create new secret key". It stays on your phone only.

💡 You can skip and add it later in Settings, but the tutor won't reply until it's in.
